Installed.

1. Open "RTL8723BE Wireless Utility" and click "Load Driver…" (asks for your password).
2. The first time (and after each update) macOS asks you to allow the driver in
   System Settings → Privacy & Security. Allow it, restart, and click "Load Driver…"
   again after logging in.
3. Click Scan, choose your network and enter its password — or in Terminal:

       rtl8723be_cli scan
       rtl8723be_cli connect "Network Name" 'password'

Load the driver again after each restart. The connection appears as a wired
network port (en*), not in the Wi-Fi menu. To remove everything, run uninstall.sh
from the disk image with sudo.
