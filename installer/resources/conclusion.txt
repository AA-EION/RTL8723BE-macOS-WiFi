Installed.

1. Open RTL8723BE Wireless Utility after login and click Load Driver….
2. If macOS requires extension approval, follow Privacy & Security and restart;
   load again after login. Do not enable boot-time injection for this build.
3. Scan, join a WPA2 Personal network and choose Remember in Keychain. Saved
   networks can auto-join while the app runs. Right-click a network to forget it.
4. Settings shows negotiated mode/offload options and the separate Bluetooth
   loader. Follow Bluetooth Guide.txt for manual loading and external dependencies;
   pairing and power controls use macOS Bluetooth Settings.

Wi-Fi remains an Ethernet-style en* interface. Full Wi-Fi 4 compliance, native
AirPort integration and Bluetooth hardware validation remain pending.
Load drivers again after a restart. Use uninstall.sh from the disk image to remove
both drivers and the app. Forget passwords before uninstalling if desired.
