Almost done.

1. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click "Allow" for the
   RTL8723BE driver if macOS asks, then restart.
2. After restarting, open "RTL8723BE Wireless Utility", click Scan, choose your
   network and enter its password — or in Terminal:

       rtl8723be_cli scan
       rtl8723be_cli connect "Network Name" 'password'

The connection appears in macOS as a wired network port (en*), not in the Wi-Fi
menu. To remove everything later, run uninstall.sh from the disk image with sudo.
