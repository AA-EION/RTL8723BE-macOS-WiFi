RTL8723BE Wi-Fi for macOS
By Issen Software Group

This installs:
  • RTL8723BE Wireless Utility.app (includes the driver)  → /Applications
  • rtl8723be_cli (command-line tool)                      → /usr/local/bin

The driver is loaded by the app after you log in. It is never installed where macOS
would start it during boot, and nothing touches NVRAM or your EFI partition.

Supported hardware: Realtek RTL8723BE PCIe Wi-Fi (PCI ID 10ec:b723), 2.4 GHz, WPA2-PSK.
Tested on an HP laptop (subsystem 103c:804c) with macOS 26 (Darwin 25) under OpenCore.

Before installing:
  • The driver only starts when the boot argument  rtl8723be_experimental=1  is set
    (add it to NVRAM boot-args in your OpenCore config.plist).
  • Kernel extension loading must be allowed by your SIP/OpenCore setup.

App and tools: MIT. Kernel driver: GPL-2.0-only (ported from Linux).
